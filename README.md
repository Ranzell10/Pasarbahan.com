# Pasarbahan.com
A marketplace system designed to connect raw material sellers with customers through a digital platform.

## Project Overview
PasarBahan.com was designed to make it easier for customers to find suitable raw materials and discover different stores in the digital era.
The platform acts as a marketplace that connects customers, sellers, and administrators in one system.

The project includes several main functions such as:
- Customer registration
- Seller/shop registration
- Product catalog management
- Raw material search
- Product ordering
- Payment processing
- Shipping selection
- Order tracking
- Product reviews
- Seller product management
- Shipping status management

## Objectives
- Connect raw material sellers with customers through a digital marketplace.
- Make it easier for customers to find suitable raw materials.
- Provide sellers with a platform to manage their shops and products.
- Simplify the raw material ordering, payment, and shipping process.
- Improve accessibility and convenience in purchasing raw materials online.

## Business Process
The main business process of PasarBahan.com involves the interaction between customers, sellers, and the platform.
Customers can search for raw materials through the product catalog, select products, place orders, choose a payment and shipping method, and provide reviews after completing a transaction.
Meanwhile, sellers can register their shops, add and manage raw material products, process orders, and update shipping information.
Administrators are responsible for managing and maintaining the product catalog and seller information within the platform.

## Project Outcome
The final result is a marketplace system concept for raw materials that connects sellers and customers through a digital platform.
PasarBahan.com is designed to provide customers with an easier way to find and purchase raw materials while providing sellers with a platform to register their shops and manage the products they sell.
